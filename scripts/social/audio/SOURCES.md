# Music for the reels

Every track here is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Each page below was
opened on 12 Oct 2026 and showed "CC0" as its only licence. No credit is
required for any of them.

Each file is the first 30 seconds of the original (leading silence removed),
brought to -16 LUFS and saved as AAC, 72 kb/s stereo.

The sounds on the slide changes (whoosh, hit) are not files: ffmpeg makes
them from noise and sine sources (reel/sound.mjs).

## Adding a track

Only CC0 or public-domain music whose licence is stated on its own page.
No commercial music, nothing that asks for credit or a licence, no
soundtrack, nothing ripped. Add the file, add it to `TRACKS` in
`reel/sound.mjs`, and add its row here. Keep the folder well under 5 MB.
