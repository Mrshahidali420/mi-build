# Lessons

## 12 Oct 2026: social posts exist to send people to the site

The first live template post had one platform name, a lot of empty space and a
two-line caption. The owner's correction, as rules:

- Every image carries the score, the genres, the status and the alt title as
  labelled lines, and one line that says the link is in the first comment.
  A scroller must get it without reading the caption.
- Every caption has a hook (an emoji or two is fine), a short synopsis, the
  key facts, a question that names the title (and the platform where it
  fits), then the line about the first comment, then hashtags built from the
  record (title, platform, season, studio, sub-genre) plus one or two
  community tags. Seven hashtags at most.
- No template may ship with dead space. Check it with one platform and with
  five before calling it done.
- Show every platform the site page lists, with the same count the page shows
  ("3 legal places · 3 free to start"), not only the English ones.
- Say the status the way readers do: Ongoing, Currently airing, Completed, On
  hiatus, Not yet released. Never "still coming out". Never claim a schedule
  the data does not hold.
- Cover art gets a thin solid line or an offset block so it stands off a dark
  page. No glow, no gradient, no pill badges.
- The site link goes first in the first comment. The Amazon line is second,
  only for a title the site has a real product for, only with the site's own
  tag, and never with the Associates sentence.
- Do not tag official pages (@Crunchyroll) from the poster.
