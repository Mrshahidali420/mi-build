One Piece has been running for close to thirty years, and its power scale has grown with it. A pirate who seemed unbeatable in the East Blue would not last a minute against an admiral, and the admirals look small next to the Emperors. This ranking lists the fifteen strongest characters in One Piece by what the manga actually shows them doing, so each placement can be checked against real scenes.

The short version: Luffy takes the top spot after beating Kaido with Gear 5, Shanks and Blackbeard follow, and Whitebeard and Kaido lead the characters who have already fallen.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the One Piece manga up to the end of the Egghead arc, including Wano, Hachinosu and Egghead.

## How this ranking works

Every character is judged by their feats on the page: who they beat, who beat them, and what they survived. When a character's strength is only talked about, as with Imu or Monkey D. Dragon, it counts for very little until the story shows it. Bounties are a useful hint but not proof, since the World Government sets them for political reasons as much as strength. For a full list of those numbers, see our guide to [One Piece bounties](/guides/one-piece-bounties).

Characters are judged at the strongest point the story has shown. For Whitebeard that is Marineford, where he was old and ill. For Luffy it is Gear 5 after Wano. When the anime and manga differ, the manga decides, and filler fights and movies are left out.

## Just outside the top 15

**Gol D. Roger** may be the strongest pirate who ever lived. He fought Whitebeard to a draw and clashed with Garp for years, but almost all of it happens in flashback, so he is hard to place. **Imu**, the hidden ruler of the World Government, and the **Five Elders** are clearly at the very top of the story's power, yet the Elders were held off at Egghead and Imu has barely fought on panel. **Monkey D. Dragon** has never been shown in a real fight.

Among the active fighters, **Marco the Phoenix** held off two of Kaido's commanders at once, **Sanji** beat Queen, **Trafalgar Law** and **Eustass Kid** took down Big Mom together, and **Sabo** now carries Ace's fire. Each of them has a strong case for the next five spots.

## The verdict

Luffy is the strongest character in One Piece as of the Egghead arc, because he is the one who beat Kaido and walked away. Shanks may be his equal and Blackbeard is the man the story keeps building toward, so the top three could still change. Below them, the Marines are led by Akainu, the swordsmen by Mihawk, and the Straw Hats' second-strongest by Zoro.

The manga is heading toward its final saga, and the biggest unknowns, Imu, the Five Elders and Dragon, will decide the last fights. When they do, this list will change with them. For now, the One Piece page on this site lists every official place to watch the anime or read the latest chapters.
