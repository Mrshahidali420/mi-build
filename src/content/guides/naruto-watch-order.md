Naruto is one of the longest anime of its era: 720 episodes across the first series and Shippuden, plus
Boruto and close to a dozen films. The order itself is simple. What makes it look hard is the filler,
the films and the question of where The Last fits.

Here is the order we recommend, what each part covers, and how to keep the filler from eating your
evenings.

## The short answer

1. Naruto (episodes 1 to 135 are the story; 136 to 220 are almost all filler)
2. Naruto Shippuden, episodes 1 to 493
3. The Last: Naruto the Movie
4. Naruto Shippuden, episodes 494 to 500
5. Boruto: Naruto the Movie, or the Boruto series' Chunin Exam arc
6. Boruto: Naruto Next Generations

## About the filler

The anime was made week by week while Masashi Kishimoto was still drawing the manga, and whenever it
caught up, the studio filled time with original stories. In the first series this happened all at once:
after Sasuke leaves the village around episode 135, there is a long run of filler before Shippuden
begins. In Shippuden the filler is scattered between canon arcs, so it is easier to miss and harder to
skip without a list.

You do not have to skip it all. A few filler arcs fill gaps the manga left open and are well liked. But
if you want the story at its own pace, skip the filler arcs and watch the mixed episodes, which carry
canon scenes alongside new ones.

## The other films

Naruto has three films from the first series and six from Shippuden, including Road to Ninja. All of
them are side adventures outside the main story. Watch them any time after the point they are set,
or skip them. Only The Last and Boruto: Naruto the Movie count as canon.

Shippuden was still on air when The Last came out in 2014, and its final arc, which shows the wedding,
aired in 2017, after the film. That is why the film goes before Shippuden's last seven episodes.

## After the anime: the manga

The Naruto anime adapts the whole of Kishimoto's 700-chapter manga, so there is nothing left of
Naruto's own story to read. The Boruto anime is another matter: it ended in 2023 while the manga
carried on. The sequel, Boruto: Two Blue Vortex, picks up after a time skip and is where the story
lives now.

## How long it takes

Naruto is 220 episodes of about 23 minutes, roughly 84 hours. Shippuden is 500 episodes, about 190 hours,
and Boruto adds another 293 episodes. Watched in full, Naruto and Shippuden alone come to more than 270
hours, which is why so many viewers use a filler list. Skipping the filler blocks takes a large bite out of
that, especially in Shippuden, without losing any of the story.
