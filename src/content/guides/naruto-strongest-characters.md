Ask ten fans who the strongest character in Naruto is and you will get ten answers, usually backed by a single fight remembered from years ago. This ranking takes the other road. It lists the fifteen most powerful characters in Naruto and Naruto Shippuden by what the story actually shows them doing, and explains each placement so you can check the same scenes yourself.

The short version: Kaguya Ōtsutsuki sits at the top, Naruto and Sasuke follow at the peak of their Six Paths power, and Madara Uchiha is the strongest villain the main cast ever has to fight. The rest of the list is where the arguments start.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole of Naruto Shippuden, including the Fourth Great Ninja War and the final battle.

## How this ranking works

Each character is judged at their peak form in the original story, Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, manga chapters 1 to 700 and the anime that adapts them. Boruto is left out. A finished story is easier to judge than one still being written, and Boruto changes the scale so much that it deserves its own list.

Placement goes by feats: who a character beat, what they survived, and what the story states plainly. Hype from other characters only counts when a fight backs it up, and databook numbers break ties at most. Anime-only filler, video games and "what if" matchups are ignored. When the anime and manga differ, the manga decides.

Two smaller rules shape the list. First, a character's best moment counts even when it is a one-off, so Might Guy's Eighth Gate is judged as Night Guy. Second, powers that are told but never shown count for less than those shown blow by blow, which is why the Sage of Six Paths ranks below characters whose top-level fights the series actually draws.

## Just outside the top 15

Several names could argue their way onto this list. **Hiruzen Sarutobi**, the Third Hokage, knew every jutsu in the Hidden Leaf and fought Orochimaru and two revived Hokage at once, but he did it as an old man and the fight cost him his life. **Jiraiya** in Sage Mode pushed Pain further than anyone before Naruto, yet still lost. **Orochimaru** is impossible to kill, though he rarely wins a fight that matters. **Gaara** shines as a commander in the war, and **Shisui Uchiha** owned the most feared genjutsu in the series, Kotoamatsukami, but we see too little of him in action to place him.

**Hamura Ōtsutsuki** and the reincarnated **Indra** and **Asura** would rank high if the story showed more of them. It shows almost nothing, so they stay off the list. The **Ten-Tails** is the most powerful creature in the series, but this is a ranking of characters who fight, and the Ten-Tails is better read as the power several of them borrow.

## The verdict

Kaguya is the strongest being in Naruto, full stop. Among the characters the story follows, Naruto edges out Sasuke because the series ends their rivalry with Naruto still standing. Below them the gap is real: Madara and Obito only reached their peaks by becoming the Ten-Tails' host, and Hashirama remains the high-water mark for a human ninja without a tailed beast or the Sage's gift.
