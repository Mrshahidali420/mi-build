The easiest anime characters to cosplay are the ones whose look comes from a handful of everyday pieces and one clear detail, such as a hairstyle, a prop or a colour. This guide picks twelve of them, none of which repeat our list of [easy anime cosplay ideas for beginners](/guides/easy-anime-cosplay-ideas), so between the two you have two dozen options. Each pick comes with the key pieces, a rough budget and tips to make it recognisable.

They are ranked from the easiest, costumes you can assemble from your own wardrobe tonight, to the ones that take a bit more shopping.

## How we picked these characters

Every character here passes three tests. The outfit is built mostly from ordinary clothes with no sewing or armour. One detail, like Light's notebook or Power's horns, makes the character obvious at a glance. And the series is popular enough that people at a convention or party will know who you are.

Budgets are in US dollars, assuming you buy everything cheaply and new. If you already own a white shirt, a suit or black trousers, the real cost drops sharply. A wig is usually the biggest single expense.

## Make it recognisable

**Hair first.** A wig in the right colour and cut does more than any other piece. Makima's braid, Power's horns and Frieren's twin tails are what people recognise, so put your budget there.

**One signature prop.** Light's notebook, Aki's katana, Shinji's headphones and Nezuko's bamboo muzzle each turn a plain outfit into a character. Pick the one prop that matters and make it well.

**Group up.** Chainsaw Man is the easiest group cosplay in anime: Aki, Makima, Power and Denji all wear the same white shirt, black tie and dark trousers. One shopping trip covers four people.

**Learn the face.** Shinji's worry, Light's smirk, Power's grin and Frieren's flat stare cost nothing and make your photos much better.

## Watch the series first

A costume is more fun when you know the character, and it helps you choose the outfit version you like most.
