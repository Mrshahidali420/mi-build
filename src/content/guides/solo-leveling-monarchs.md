The Monarchs are the force behind everything that goes wrong in Solo Leveling. The gates, the dungeon breaks and the monsters that kill hunters are all part of their war, and Sung Jinwoo's power comes from one of them. This guide explains who the nine Monarchs are, what each one commands, how they relate to the Rulers, and how each one falls in the manhwa.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole Solo Leveling manhwa, including the war with the Monarchs and the ending.

## What the Monarchs are

The Monarchs are nine kings of darkness, each ruling a race of monsters: dragons, giants, beasts, insects, demons, frost elves and more. Opposite them stand the Rulers, beings of light. The two sides have been at war since long before humans existed, and the gates that open on Earth are a symptom of that war spilling into the human world.

Each Monarch has a title tied to their power and a second title as king of their race. Ashborn, for example, is the Monarch of Shadows and the King of the Dead. To act on Earth, the Monarchs and Rulers often work through vessels, humans who can carry a fragment of their power. Jinwoo is the most important vessel of all, chosen by Ashborn himself.

## Where the System comes from

The System that turns Jinwoo from the weakest hunter into a player who can level up is not a gift from nowhere. It was built to prepare a vessel for the Shadow Monarch. Every quest, penalty zone and level-up is training for a body strong enough to hold Ashborn's power, which is why the System can push Jinwoo so hard and why it ends when the training is done.

## The Rulers and the Absolute Being

The Rulers are the Monarchs' sworn enemies, beings of light who need human vessels to fight on Earth. Jinwoo's father, Sung Il-hwan, returns from the world beyond the gates as one of their vessels and holds off two Monarchs by himself before the strain kills him. In the final war the Rulers fight beside Jinwoo, and it is their attack from behind that lets him break Antares.

Above both sides is the **Absolute Being**, the creator who stands behind the Rulers and the Monarchs alike. The story keeps him mostly in the background until the end, where his place in the endless war is finally made clear.

## Ranking the Monarchs

The story makes the top of the order plain. Ashborn is the strongest of the original nine, and Jinwoo inherits that strength and grows past it. Antares is the strongest of the Monarchs who stay enemies, and the only one who needs Jinwoo and the Rulers together to stop. The other seven are broadly peers. Each one is far beyond any human hunter, and each one falls to Jinwoo or his army in a fight the story keeps short, which is how Solo Leveling shows how far its hero has climbed.

## The ending

After Antares falls, Jinwoo uses the Cup of Reincarnation to turn back time. In the new timeline he fights the Monarchs' war on his own, out of sight, so that the gates never open and the people who died the first time live. It is why the ending feels quiet: almost nobody on Earth knows the war happened.
