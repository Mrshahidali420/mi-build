Every action anime needs rules for its powers. Some keep them loose, but the best series build a full system: where power comes from, how it grows, what it costs and how it can be beaten. Those rules are what make a fight feel earned instead of random. This guide explains the biggest anime power systems in plain terms, one by one.

## What makes a good power system

A strong power system does three things. It has **a clear source**, so you know where power comes from. It has **limits and costs**, so even the strongest characters can lose. And it leaves **room for cleverness**, so a weaker fighter can win by thinking. The systems fans love most, such as Nen and cursed energy, do all three.

## Patterns across series

Look at these systems side by side and the same ideas keep coming back. **Life energy** sits at the core of chakra, Nen, ki and reiatsu. **Costs and vows** appear in Nen, cursed energy and Demon Slayer's marks, because a power with a price creates drama. **Inherited gifts** show up as kekkei genkai, Quirks, cursed techniques and Devil Fruit bloodlines. And **transformations**, from Bankai to Super Saiyan to Gear 5, give the story big moments to build toward.

That shared language is also what makes cross-series arguments so tempting, and so hard. Each system measures power differently, which is why careful rankings stay inside one series. The guide to power scaling explains how fans compare characters anyway.

## Where to start

If you want the most satisfying system, watch Hunter x Hunter. If you want the most stylish, watch Jujutsu Kaisen. If you want the most inventive, watch JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. Each series page on this site lists the official places to watch the anime or read the manga.
