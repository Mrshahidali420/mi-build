Solo Leveling is a story about one man outgrowing everyone else, which makes the top of any ranking easy. The interesting part is everything underneath him: the Monarchs, the shadow soldiers and the national level hunters who are the best humanity has. This ranking places the fifteen strongest characters in Solo Leveling by what the manhwa shows them doing, with a short reason for each spot.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole Solo Leveling manhwa, including the war with the Monarchs and the ending. The anime has not reached most of it yet.

## How this ranking works

Characters are judged by their best showing in the main Solo Leveling manhwa, the comic adaptation that ends with the war against the Monarchs. The side story and the spin-off Solo Leveling: Ragnarok are left out so the list judges one finished story.

Placement goes by feats: who a character beat, who beat them, and what the story states plainly about their rank. Hunter ranks and titles count when the fights back them up. When the story says little about a character's power but places them high, such as Christopher Reed, the entry says so.

The Rulers, the beings who fought the Monarchs since the beginning, are left off the main list because they mostly act together and through human vessels, which makes them hard to rank one by one.

## Just outside the top 15

**The other Monarchs** would all rank inside the top ten if they were included one by one. Frost Monarch Sillad, Beast Monarch Rakan, Plague Monarch Querehsha, Legia, Tarnak, Yogumunt and Baran are each a match for the strongest humans, and each one dies to Jinwoo or his army. They have their own guide, Solo Leveling Monarchs explained.

**Kamish**, the dragon it took a raid of the world's best hunters to bring down years before the story, is the benchmark for a disaster-class monster, and Jinwoo later raises it as a shadow. **Tusk**, **Iron** and **Tank** are useful shadows but nowhere near the marshals. Among the Korean hunters, **Yoo Jinho** is a D-rank with a huge heart and almost no power, and **Woo Jinchul** is a tough A-rank inspector who is better at judging power than holding it.

## The verdict

Jinwoo is the strongest character in Solo Leveling by a margin the story is happy to show. Below him, Ashborn and Antares sit in a class of their own, one the source of Jinwoo's power and the other the last enemy that power has to overcome. The shadow army's marshals come next, then the national level hunters, who are the peak of humanity and still far below the Monarchs.
