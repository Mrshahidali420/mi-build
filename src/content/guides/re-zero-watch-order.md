Re:Zero is a straight line with two detours. The TV seasons follow the light novels in order, so the main
question is where to put the two OVAs, and whether to watch the original first season or the Director's
Cut that replaced it on many services.

## The short answer

1. Re:Zero Season 1 (or the Director's Cut)
2. The OVAs: Memory Snow, then The Frozen Bond
3. Season 2
4. Season 2 Part 2
5. Season 3
6. Season 4

## Season 1 or the Director's Cut?

In 2020, before Season 2, Season 1 was re-released as a Director's Cut: the same 25 episodes joined into
13 hour-long episodes, with some scenes added or changed. Some services carry
only one version. Both tell the same story, so watch whichever you can find and move on.

## Where the OVAs go

The two OVAs were released between Season 1 and Season 2, and that is still the best place for them.
Memory Snow is pure comedy, set in a calm stretch of Season 1, and you can skip it without losing anything.
The Frozen Bond is a prequel about Emilia's past with Puck, and Season 2 leans on that history, so it is
worth the hour.

## What you can skip

- **Re:Zero Break Time.** Short chibi comedy episodes that ran alongside each season. Fun, not story.
- **Memory Snow,** if you are short on time. The Frozen Bond is the one to keep.
- **The Director's Cut,** if you have seen the original Season 1, or the other way round.

## What each season adapts

Re:Zero is based on Tappei Nagatsuki's light novels, which are split into numbered arcs. Season 1 adapts
arcs one to three: Subaru's first day in the capital, the Roswaal mansion, and the fight for Emilia's place
in the royal selection, ending with the White Whale and the Witch Cult. Season 2, in both parts, is the
fourth arc, the Sanctuary. Season 3 is the fifth arc, the battle for Priestella. Season 4 carries on from
there.

## How long it takes

Season 1 is 25 episodes, about ten and a half hours with its double-length opener. The two OVAs add a
little over two hours. Season 2 Part 1 is 13 episodes and Part 2 is 12, together around 11 and a half hours.
Season 3 is 16 episodes, a little over six hours, and Season 4 is 19 episodes, about seven and a half. In
all, the anime so far is close to 38 hours.

## A note on the tone

Re:Zero is a fantasy story, but it is not a light one. Subaru's power means he dies, often and painfully,
and each death is shown and remembered. Season 1 eases you in for a few episodes before it shows what the
series is really about. If the first arc feels slow, keep going to the mansion arc: that is where most
viewers get hooked.
