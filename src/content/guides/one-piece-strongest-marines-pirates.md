One Piece has two sides at war, the pirates and the Marines, and fans have argued for decades about who sits at the top of each. This ranking puts both on one list. It places the fifteen strongest pirates and Marines in One Piece by what the manga and anime show them doing, and notes each one's side, bounty or rank and best feat.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers One Piece up to the Elbaph arc, including Wano, Egghead and the events at Hachinosu.

## How this ranking works

Every character is judged at their peak. That means Roger, Whitebeard and Garp count at their best, not as old men or ghosts. The ranking uses fights shown in the manga and anime, with the story's own statements, such as Kaido being called the strongest creature in the world, used only when fights back them up.

Bounties are listed but do not decide the order. A bounty reflects the threat a pirate poses to the World Government, not only fighting strength, which is why Buggy is an Emperor and not on this list. Anime-only filler and the films are ignored. One Piece is still running, so the present-day names may move as the final saga goes on.

## Just outside the top 15

**Charlotte Katakuri**, Big Mom's strongest son, can see a few seconds into the future and gave Luffy his hardest fight before Wano. **Marco the Phoenix**, Whitebeard's first mate, holds off two Wano bosses together. **Silvers Rayleigh**, Roger's right-hand man, is still fearsome in old age. **Monkey D. Dragon**, Luffy's father and leader of the Revolutionary Army, would likely rank high, but the story has not shown him fight. **Koby** is the Marine to watch, growing fast since the time skip.

The **Five Elders** and **Imu** are left off because they are neither pirates nor Marines, and their full power is still being revealed.

## The verdict

Roger and Whitebeard are the two strongest pirates One Piece has shown, and no living character has yet clearly passed them. Today's top tier is Luffy, Shanks and Blackbeard, the Emperors heading for a final clash. On the Marine side, Garp at his peak stands above every admiral, while Akainu is the strongest Marine still on active duty.
