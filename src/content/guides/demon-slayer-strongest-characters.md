Demon Slayer is a story about ordinary people chasing a monster who is far stronger than any of them. That makes ranking its characters unusually clear: the manga is finished, almost every top fighter faces the same enemies, and the final battle puts nearly all of them on the same field. This list ranks the fifteen strongest characters in Demon Slayer, slayers and demons together, by what they actually do on the page.

In short: Yoriichi sits alone at the top, Muzan and Kokushibo follow, and Tanjiro is the strongest of the living slayers by the last chapter.

> Spoiler note: this ranking covers the complete manga, up to and including the final battle against Muzan.

## How this ranking works

Every character is judged at their peak in the manga. Feats decide the order: who a character beat, who they lost to, and what they survived. Two power-ups from the final arc matter a lot here, the Demon Slayer Mark and the red blade, so characters who died before those existed, like Rengoku, sit lower than they would if they had lived. Statements count when a fight backs them up. When the anime and the manga differ, the manga decides.

Demons are ranked by their real fights, not just their number in the Twelve Kizuki. The numbers are a good guide, but Muzan's ranking of his own demons is about usefulness as well as strength.

## Just outside the top 15

**Mitsuri Kanroji** held off Hantengu's strongest clone and fought Muzan in the final battle; she would be sixteenth. **Gyokko** and **Nakime** both held Upper Rank seats, and Nakime's control of the Infinity Castle shaped the whole final arc. **Inosuke** and **Kanao** helped finish Doma, and **Genya Shinazugawa** played a key part against Kokushibo by eating pieces of demons to borrow their powers.

**Shinobu Kocho** deserves a mention for one of the most important acts in the story: her poison made Doma beatable. And **Tamayo**, a doctor rather than a fighter, created the drug that turned Muzan into an old man in the final battle. Without her, the sunrise might never have come in time.

## The verdict

Yoriichi is the strongest character in Demon Slayer, and the story treats him as a once-in-history talent. Muzan is the strongest demon and only loses because the entire Corps buys time until dawn. Kokushibo is the deadliest of the Upper Ranks, Tanjiro becomes the strongest living slayer, and Gyomei leads the Hashira.
