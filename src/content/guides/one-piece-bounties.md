A bounty in One Piece is the price the World Government puts on a criminal's head, and it is the fastest way to see how much of a threat the world thinks someone is. This guide lists the highest bounties in One Piece, explains what the numbers do and do not tell you, and walks through Luffy's bounty from his first 30 million to his current 3 billion.

The short answer: Gol D. Roger has the highest bounty ever revealed at 5,564,800,000 berries. Among active pirates, Shanks leads at 4,048,900,000, and Luffy's current bounty is 3,000,000,000.

> Spoiler note: all figures are as of the Egghead arc of the manga, including the bounties revealed after Wano.

## How bounties work

The World Government sets each bounty based on the danger a person poses to the government, not purely on fighting strength. That is why Buggy is priced above Luffy, and why Nico Robin was wanted for 79 million berries at the age of eight for being able to read the Poneglyphs. Most posters say "Dead or Alive". A rare few say "Only Alive", as Sanji's does, when the government wants the person taken alive for a reason of its own.

Bounties also freeze. Warlords had their bounties suspended while they served, and many came back much higher when the system was abolished after the Levely. A pirate who dies keeps their final bounty, which is why Roger and Whitebeard still top the list.

## The highest bounties in One Piece

The list ranks every bounty we can confirm from the manga or the official Vivre Card data book. The figures for Roger, Whitebeard, Kaido and Big Mom are their last known bounties.

Two of Kaido's commanders also cleared a billion: **King** at 1,390,000,000 and **Queen** at 1,320,000,000. King would sit between Hancock and Marco on this list, and Queen between Marco and Zoro. Just under the billion-berry line come **Charlotte Smoothie** at 932,000,000 and **Nico Robin** at 930,000,000.

## Luffy's bounty history

Luffy's bounty has gone up seven times. Each raise follows a fight that embarrassed the government or one of its allies.

| Arc | Bounty (berries) | Why it went up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arlong Park | 30,000,000 | Beat Arlong, Buggy and Don Krieg, the top pirates in the East Blue |
| Alabasta | 100,000,000 | Defeated the Warlord Crocodile |
| Enies Lobby | 300,000,000 | Declared war on the World Government to save Robin |
| Marineford | 400,000,000 | Broke into Impel Down and the war at Marineford |
| Dressrosa | 500,000,000 | Defeated the Warlord Doflamingo |
| Whole Cake Island | 1,500,000,000 | Beat Katakuri and escaped Big Mom |
| Wano | 3,000,000,000 | Defeated Kaido and became an Emperor |

## The Straw Hat crew's bounties

As of the Egghead arc the full crew's bounties are: Luffy 3,000,000,000, Zoro 1,111,000,000, Jinbe 1,100,000,000, Sanji 1,032,000,000, Robin 930,000,000, Usopp 500,000,000, Franky 394,000,000, Brook 383,000,000, Nami 366,000,000 and Chopper 1,000. Together that is 8,816,001,000 berries. Usopp's poster now carries the epithet "God", earned in Dressrosa.

## Bounty is not strength

Treat bounties as a rough guide only. Buggy outranks Luffy, Chopper is valued at a thousand berries, and Blackbeard had no bounty at all for most of his life.
