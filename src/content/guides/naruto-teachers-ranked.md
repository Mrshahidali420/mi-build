Naruto starts the series as the worst student in his class, and by the end he is the strongest ninja alive. That climb took a lot of teachers. Some taught him techniques, some taught him how to think, and one simply believed in him first. This guide ranks Naruto's teachers by how much they shaped him, and says what each one taught and when.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers Naruto and Naruto Shippuden in full.

## How this ranking works

The order goes by impact on Naruto as a ninja and as a person: what each teacher gave him, how long they spent with him, and how much of his final strength traces back to them. Strength as a fighter does not decide the order, so a weaker ninja who changed Naruto's life can rank above a stronger one who taught him one trick. Filler training arcs are left out.

## Honourable mentions

**Kurama**, the Nine-Tails, ends the series as Naruto's partner rather than his teacher, but their bond is the final lesson of Shippuden. **Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki**, the Sage of Six Paths, gives Naruto the power he uses to fight Kaguya, which is a gift, not training. **Gamabunta** and the toads teach Naruto plenty about summoning the hard way. And **Sasuke**, never a teacher to him, pushes Naruto harder than any sensei by being the rival he cannot let go.

## Naruto's training, in story order

It helps to see the teachers in the order Naruto met them, because each lesson builds on the last.

**At the Academy,** Iruka teaches him the basics and, more importantly, gives him the first bond he ever has. Naruto fails the graduation exam three times, and it is only after he learns the Multi Shadow Clone Jutsu from the forbidden scroll and protects Iruka from Mizuki that he passes.

**On Team 7,** Kakashi takes over. The bell test teaches teamwork, and tree climbing in the Land of Waves gives Naruto the chakra control he lacked. Kakashi also hands him a habit he never drops: use shadow clones to do more than one person can.

**Before the Chunin Exam finals,** Ebisu starts teaching water walking, and Jiraiya takes over. Jiraiya shows Naruto how to pull on the Nine-Tails' chakra on purpose and teaches him to summon toads, which he uses to fight Gaara's Shukaku. On the search for Tsunade, Jiraiya teaches him the Rasengan, the technique that becomes Naruto's signature.

**Between the two series,** Jiraiya takes him on a two-and-a-half-year journey. The anime skips most of it, but Naruto returns faster, stronger and with far better control.

**In Shippuden,** Kakashi and Yamato guide the wind nature training that produces the Rasenshuriken. After Jiraiya's death, Fukasaku teaches him Sage Mode at Mount Myoboku, and Naruto uses it to beat Pain. During that fight, his father's chakra stops him from releasing the Nine-Tails.

**Before the war,** Killer Bee takes Naruto through the Waterfall of Truth and on to the struggle for the Nine-Tails' chakra, where his mother, Kushina, helps him win. Everything Naruto does in the Fourth Great Ninja War grows out of that moment.

## Kakashi or Jiraiya?

Fans argue about which of the two was Naruto's real teacher. Kakashi was assigned to him and taught him how to work with others, which is the value the whole series rests on. Jiraiya chose him, spent years with him one on one and taught him the techniques he fought with. This ranking puts Jiraiya first because Naruto's strength and his dream of peace both come most directly from him, but there is no wrong answer: Naruto himself treats both as family.

## What each teacher gave him

Put together, the list shows how Naruto was built. Iruka gave him a reason to keep going. Kakashi gave him a team. Jiraiya gave him the Rasengan and a dream to carry on. Fukasaku gave him Sage Mode, Killer Bee gave him the Nine-Tails' power, and his parents gave him the answer to the one question he never stopped asking: who he was.
