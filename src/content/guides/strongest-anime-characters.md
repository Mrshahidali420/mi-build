Ask who the strongest anime character is and you are really asking how to compare stories that were never meant to meet. Dragon Ball counts in universes, One Piece in islands, and Jujutsu Kaisen in city blocks. This list ranks fifteen of the most powerful characters in anime by one simple measure: the biggest thing their series actually shows them doing.

The short answer: Zeno from Dragon Ball Super sits at the top, followed by Ultimate Madoka and Simon from Gurren Lagann. Goku and Saitama, the two names most people expect, sit just behind the gods of their own worlds.

> Spoiler note: this list uses each series up to its latest finished arc, including the endings of Jujutsu Kaisen, Bleach, Hunter x Hunter's Chimera Ant arc and Solo Leveling.

## How this ranking works

Each character is placed by scale first. A character who destroys a planet on screen outranks one who destroys a city, and a character who rewrites reality outranks both. Within the same scale, the order comes from who beat whom inside their own series.

Three rules keep the list fair. Feats must be shown, not just claimed. Video games, non-canon movies and fan theories are ignored. And each series is judged by its own canon: the anime where it is the main version, the manga where the manga goes further.

## The tiers in plain words

**Beyond the universe:** Zeno, Ultimate Madoka and Simon. Their series show them acting on many universes or on reality itself.

**Universe level:** Beerus and Goku. Dragon Ball Super tells us a full fight between gods could end a universe, and shows enough to back it up.

**Planet and world level:** Saitama, Yhwach, Sung Jinwoo and Kaguya. Each can threaten or reshape an entire world.

**Top of their own world:** Aizen, Luffy, Meruem, Sukuna, Gojo and Mob. Their feats are smaller in size, but each is at or near the peak of a series that millions follow.

## Just outside the list

**Whis** and **Grand Priest** from Dragon Ball Super are stronger than Beerus but barely fight. **Rimuru Tempest** from That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime grows to a scale far beyond this list in the light novels, but the anime has not reached it. **Giorno Giovanna's** Gold Experience Requiem and **Anos Voldigoad** from The Misfit of Demon King Academy are popular picks whose powers are hard to measure fairly against the rest.

## The verdict

Zeno is the strongest anime character the mainstream screen has shown. Below him, the list is a ladder of scale, and every character on it is the strongest, or close to it, in their own story.
