The Hashira are the reason Demon Slayer feels so large. Tanjiro starts the story as a beginner, and the nine Hashira are the wall of skill he has to grow into. This guide explains what a Hashira is, how a slayer earns the title, and then ranks all nine by what they do on the page, with each one's Breathing style, best fight and fate.

The quick answer: Gyomei Himejima, the Stone Hashira, is the strongest, with Sanemi and Giyu close behind.

> Spoiler note: the ranking uses the whole manga, including the Infinity Castle and the final battle against Muzan, which the anime is still adapting.

## What is a Hashira?

Hashira means "pillar". The Hashira are the nine highest-ranking swordsmen of the Demon Slayer Corps, the people who hold up the whole organisation. Each one has mastered a Breathing style, usually one of the five main styles (Water, Flame, Wind, Stone and Thunder) or a style that grew out of them, such as Love from Flame or Mist from Wind. They answer to Kagaya Ubuyashiki, the head of the Corps, and they are the ones sent after the Twelve Kizuki, Muzan's strongest demons.

The number nine is fixed. When a Hashira dies or retires, a seat opens and a new slayer can take it.

## How a slayer becomes a Hashira

There are two requirements. The slayer must reach Kinoe, the top of the Corps' ten ranks. And they must either kill one of the Twelve Kizuki or kill fifty demons. Muichiro reached the title two months after first holding a sword, which is why the others treat him as a prodigy.

The previous generation is part of the story too. Sakonji Urokodaki, Tanjiro's teacher, was a Water Hashira. Rengoku's father, Shinjuro, was the Flame Hashira before him. Kanae Kocho, Shinobu's older sister, was the Flower Hashira until Doma killed her. And Jigoro Kuwajima, who trained Zenitsu, was a Thunder Hashira.

## How this ranking works

Placement goes by feats in the manga: which demon each Hashira fought, how the fight went, and whether they awakened the Demon Slayer Mark or turned their blade red, the two power-ups of the final arc. Statements from other characters count when a fight backs them up, as with Kokushibo's praise of Gyomei. When the anime and manga differ, the manga decides.

## The Mark and the red blade

Two power-ups explain most of the gap between the top five and the rest. The **Demon Slayer Mark** appears when a slayer pushes their heart rate and body heat to a deadly level; it raises their strength and speed but shortens their life to around twenty-five years. The **red blade**, or Crimson Nichirin Blade, is a sword heated by sheer grip strength until it stops demons from regenerating. Rengoku, Tengen and Shinobu fought before either power-up was known, which is a big reason they rank lower. Had Rengoku lived to train with them, he might well sit higher.

## The verdict

Gyomei is the strongest Hashira, and the story leaves little doubt about it. Sanemi and Giyu form the next tier, then Obanai and Muichiro, who both reached their peak in the final arc. Rengoku and Tengen are the strongest of the Hashira who never had the Mark, and Mitsuri and Shinobu round out the list with fights that mattered more than their rank suggests.
