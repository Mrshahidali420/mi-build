Dragon Ball covers five TV series, a re-edit of one of them and more than twenty films, which makes it
look harder to start than it is. The main story is three series long: Dragon Ball, Dragon Ball Z and
Dragon Ball Super, with two recent films that carry on from Super. The rest is optional.

This guide gives the order to watch in, what each series covers, which films count, and where to read
on once the anime runs out.

## The short answer

1. Dragon Ball
2. Dragon Ball Z, or Dragon Ball Z Kai instead
3. Dragon Ball Super
4. Dragon Ball Super: Broly
5. Dragon Ball Super: SUPER HERO
6. Dragon Ball DAIMA (or after Z)
7. Dragon Ball GT, only if you want more

## Z or Kai?

Dragon Ball Z was made at the same time as the manga, and when the anime caught up, the studio slowed
it down with long stares, extra fights and filler arcs. Kai, made twenty years later, cuts most of that
out. Many first-time viewers today pick Kai and do not miss much. If you have the time and like the
original voices and music, Z is the full experience. Both lead into Super the same way.

## The films

Most of the Dragon Ball Z films were made as side stories that do not fit anywhere in the timeline,
including the three original Broly films. They are fun on their own, but you lose nothing by skipping
them. A few worth knowing about:

- **Dragon Ball Z: Battle of Gods** (2013) and **Resurrection 'F'** (2015) are canon, but Dragon Ball
  Super retells both in its first two arcs. Watch either the films or those episodes.
- **The History of Trunks** (1993) is a TV special that tells future Trunks' story in full. It adds weight
  to the Android arc.
- **Dragon Ball Super: Broly** and **Super Hero** are part of the main story and come after Super.

## After the anime: the manga

The original Dragon Ball manga by Akira Toriyama is fully adapted by Dragon Ball and Z, so there is
nothing left to read there except the art. Dragon Ball Super is different. Its manga, drawn by Toyotarou
from Toriyama's outlines, keeps going past the Tournament of Power with the Galactic Patrol Prisoner
(Moro) arc and then the Granolah arc, neither of which the anime has covered. If you finish Super and want
more of the main story, that is where to go.
