Monogatari is one long story told out of order on purpose. Arcs jump back to spring break, forward to
graduation and sideways into what another character was doing the same week, and the anime followed the
order the novels came out in, not the order things happen. That is why a watch order matters more here
than for most series.

The good news: release order works almost perfectly. There is one change we recommend, which is moving
the Kizumonogatari films up to just after Bakemonogatari, and the reasons are below.

## The short answer

1. Bakemonogatari
2. Kizumonogatari Parts 1, 2 and 3
3. Nisemonogatari
4. Nekomonogatari Black
5. Monogatari Series Second Season
6. Hanamonogatari
7. Tsukimonogatari
8. Owarimonogatari
9. Koyomimonogatari
10. Owarimonogatari Second Season
11. Zoku Owarimonogatari
12. Monogatari Series: Off and Monster Season

## Why move Kizumonogatari up

Kizumonogatari was the second novel, but its films took until 2016 and 2017 to arrive. By then fans had watched five more
seasons that kept pointing back at it. Bakemonogatari refers to spring break, to Shinobu's past and to
Oshino's role in it without ever showing them, and the films are where those scenes finally appear.
Seeing them second lets the rest of the series build on them, the way the novels intended.

## A word on story order

In the order events happen, Kizumonogatari comes first, then Nekomonogatari Black, then
Bakemonogatari, and Hanamonogatari lands near the very end. It is tempting to watch it that way. Do not,
at least not the first time. Several arcs exist to show you something the earlier ones hid, and they only
work if you saw the earlier version first.

## How long it takes

The full series as listed here is about 52 hours. The biggest single piece is Second Season, 26 episodes,
around 11 hours. Bakemonogatari is about six hours, Nisemonogatari four and a half, the Kizumonogatari
trilogy about three and a half, Owarimonogatari nearly five, and Off and Monster Season around five and a
half. The rest are short: Nekomonogatari Black, Hanamonogatari and Tsukimonogatari are under two hours
each, and Koyomimonogatari's twelve episodes are only about 14 minutes apiece.
