The best first cosplay is one people recognise from across a room and you can wear all day without fixing it every ten minutes. Every idea in this guide meets both tests. They are ranked from easiest to most involved, with the key pieces, a realistic budget and how long each one takes to put together.

Most of these costumes start from ordinary clothes you may already own. Where a piece has to be bought, it is the kind of thing found in a supermarket, a thrift shop or a general online store, not a specialist prop maker.

## How we picked these costumes

Each character had to pass three checks. The outfit has to be built mostly from everyday clothing, with no armour, no large props and no sewing skills needed. The character has to be recognisable from their outfit and hair alone, so people can name you without a sign. And the series has to be popular enough that fans will spot the reference at a convention, a party or a school event.

Budgets assume you buy every piece new and cheaply, in US dollars. If you already own the shirt, jeans or dress, the real cost drops a lot. Difficulty is rated for a complete beginner with no sewing or crafting experience.

## Tips for your first cosplay

**Start with the silhouette and the hair.** People recognise a character first by shape and colour, then by detail. A good wig in the right colour does more than an expensive jacket, so if the budget is tight, spend it on the hair.

**Thrift before you buy.** Plain shirts, black trousers, pleated skirts and dark dresses turn up in second-hand shops for very little. Buy the closest match and adjust it rather than paying for a full costume set.

**Practise the pose.** L's crouch, Saitama's blank stare, Bocchi's nervous hunch and Hinata's jump are what turn clothes into a character. A minute in front of a mirror before the event makes your photos far better.

**Plan for comfort.** Wear shoes you can walk in all day and bring safety pins, a small hair brush for the wig and a bottle of water. A costume that still looks right in the evening beats one that looked perfect for the first hour.

## Watch the series first

A cosplay is more fun when you know the character, and it helps you pick the version of the outfit you like best. Each character above links to their profile and to the series page on this site, which lists the official places to watch or read it. If you are looking for something to wear next, the anime with the most memorable outfits tend to be the ones people already love, so the most popular series are a safe place to start.
