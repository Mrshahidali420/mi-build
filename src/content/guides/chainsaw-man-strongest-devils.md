Every devil in Chainsaw Man is born from a human fear, and the more people fear its name, the stronger it becomes. That simple rule produces a cast where a tiny chainsaw dog outranks almost everything in Hell. This ranking places the twelve strongest devils in Chainsaw Man by what the manga shows them doing, in both Part 1 and Part 2.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole of Chainsaw Man Part 1 and Part 2 so far, including the ending of Part 1 and the identity of the Control Devil.

## How devils get their power

A devil's strength comes from fear of its name. Fear of guns makes the Gun Devil enormous, while a devil named after something trivial stays weak. Devils who die in Hell are reborn on Earth, and the reverse. The **Primal Fears**, devils of the oldest instincts such as darkness and falling, have never died at all, which is why they are so far ahead.

Some devils live on in human bodies. **Fiends** are devils who took over a corpse and keep only part of their power. **Hybrids** such as Denji and Reze are humans who can become a devil at will and cannot die in the normal way.

## How this ranking works

Devils are ranked by feats on the page in the Chainsaw Man manga, Part 1 and Part 2 up to the latest chapters. Statements by other devils count when a scene backs them up. Part 2 is still running, so its newest devils may move as the story goes on.

## Devils, fiends and hybrids compared

The three kinds of devil-powered characters in Chainsaw Man work very differently, and it changes how they rank.

**Full devils** keep all of their power. A devil's strength depends on its name, and when it dies in Hell it is reborn on Earth with the same name, though not the same memories. That is why the Primal Fears, who have never died, are so far above the rest.

**Fiends** are devils that take over a human corpse. They keep part of their power and gain a human body, but they are much weaker than the devil they came from. Power is the best example: the Blood Devil was strong, the Blood Fiend is a handful.

**Hybrids** are humans who can become a devil at will, such as Denji with Pochita's heart and Reze with the Bomb Devil. They heal from almost anything as long as they get blood, which makes them hard to kill even when they are not the strongest fighters in the room.

Contracts add a fourth layer. Humans can trade something, a body part, years of life or a memory, for a devil's power. Aki's deals with the Fox and Curse Devils and his contract with the Future Devil show how much a hunter can borrow, and how much it costs.

## Just outside the list

**The Death Devil**, the last of the Four Horsemen, would likely rank near the top, but the series has not shown it yet. **Santa Claus** is a human contractor, not a devil, though her contract with the Darkness Devil made her one of Part 1's deadliest threats. **Kishibe** is the strongest human devil hunter, and **Quanxi**, the first devil hunter, is a hybrid of great skill. The **Ghost, Fox and Snake Devils** are useful contracts but nowhere near the names above.

## The verdict

The Chainsaw Devil is the strongest devil in Chainsaw Man, and the story is built around everyone who wants his power. Makima, the Control Devil, is second, the only one who came close to taking it. Below them, the Primal Fears are a class apart, and the Gun Devil shows how much damage a devil born from a modern fear can do.
