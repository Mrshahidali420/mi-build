Jujutsu Kaisen ended with the strongest characters in the series all fighting the same enemy, which makes it one of the easiest stories to rank at the top and one of the hardest in the middle. This ranking places the fifteen strongest characters in Jujutsu Kaisen by what the finished manga shows them doing, with the reason for each spot.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole Jujutsu Kaisen manga, including the Culling Game and the final battle with Sukuna. The anime has not reached all of it yet.

## How this ranking works

Every character is judged at their peak in the main Jujutsu Kaisen manga and Jujutsu Kaisen 0. Placement goes by feats: who they beat, who beat them, and what they did in the final battle, where most of the top names fight the same opponent. Statements from strong characters count when the fights support them.

Grades help but do not decide anything. Maki and Toji have no grade worth mentioning and rank above special grade curses, because their fights say so.

## Just outside the top 15

**Megumi Fushiguro** would rank high if Sukuna had not taken his body; his Ten Shadows is the technique Sukuna chose over all others. **Uraume**, Sukuna's loyal servant, holds off Hakari. **Naoya Zenin** is fast enough to trouble most grade 1 sorcerers, until Maki. **Kento Nanami** and **Mei Mei** are excellent grade 1 sorcerers who never quite reach the level above. **Mahoraga**, the shikigami that adapts to anything, is the key to Sukuna beating Gojo, but it is a tool rather than a fighter.

## How the final battle settles the order

Most of the top of this list is decided by one long fight. After Gojo falls, the remaining sorcerers take on Sukuna in waves at Shinjuku: Kashimo first, then Higuruma and Yuji, and later Yuta, Maki, Todo and others. Each one lands something before falling back, and the story keeps track of what each hit costs Sukuna. That makes the final battle the clearest measuring stick the series has.

It also explains why Yuji sits above Yuta. Yuta's work in Gojo's body does real damage, but Yuji's soul strikes are what separate Sukuna from Megumi, and Yuji is still standing at the end. Maki ranks above Toji for the same reason: she trades blows with Sukuna himself, while Toji's best fights came against younger or weaker foes.

## The verdict

Sukuna is the strongest character in Jujutsu Kaisen, and Gojo is the strongest sorcerer, separated by one fight the series spent years building. Yuji, the boy who swallowed a finger in the first chapter, ends the story as the one who finishes the King of Curses. Below them, Yuta and Kenjaku are a clear tier, followed by the special grade Yuki and the two people who need no cursed energy at all, Maki and Toji.
