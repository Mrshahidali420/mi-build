The Titans in Attack on Titan start as mindless monsters eating people outside the Walls. The story's first big twist is that some Titans are people, and that nine of them carry special powers handed down for two thousand years. This guide explains the nine Titans one by one: what each can do, how tall it is, and who held it.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole of Attack on Titan, including the final season and the ending.

## How the nine Titans work

All Titan power comes from Ymir Fritz, the first Titan. After her death it was split into nine, and each piece passes from holder to holder. A holder can turn into their Titan at will by injuring themselves with a clear goal in mind, and can return to human form by leaving the Titan's nape.

To inherit a Titan, an Eldian turned into a mindless Titan must eat the current holder. If a holder dies any other way, the power passes to an Eldian baby born at that moment. Every holder lives only about thirteen years after inheriting, a limit known as the curse of Ymir.

For most of history, the nation of Marley held seven of the nine. The Founding Titan stayed with the royal family inside the Walls, and the Attack Titan was in the hands of the Eldian resistance.

## Shifters and mindless Titans

It is easy to mix up the nine Titans with the Titans that roam outside the Walls. Those mindless Titans are Eldians turned by Titan spinal fluid, with no control over what they do and an urge to eat people. They cannot think, cannot change back and can be any height. A shifter who holds one of the nine keeps their mind, chooses when to transform, and can heal from wounds that would kill a human. The only way for a mindless Titan to become human again is to eat a shifter, which is how Ymir, Armin and others gained their powers.

## Ranking the nine

The Founding Titan is the strongest by a long way, because its power touches every Eldian and every other Titan, and its full use ends the world as the characters know it. Among the rest, the **War Hammer** is the hardest to beat in a duel, the **Attack Titan** the best all-round fighter, and the **Colossal** the most destructive. The **Armored** and **Female** are the best balanced between offence and defence, the **Beast** is the deadliest at range, and the **Jaw** and **Cart** trade raw power for speed and stamina.
