Demon Slayer looks simple until you meet the Mugen Train question. The story runs in a straight line,
with each arc released as its own season, but one of those arcs came out as a film first and then again
as a TV series, and the final arc is being told as a film trilogy. Here is the order and what to do about
both.

## The short answer

1. Demon Slayer Season 1
2. Mugen Train (the film, or the TV arc)
3. Entertainment District Arc
4. Swordsmith Village Arc
5. Hashira Training Arc
6. Infinity Castle (film)

## The Mugen Train question

Mugen Train was released in cinemas in 2020, between Season 1 and the Entertainment District Arc, and it
became one of the highest-grossing anime films ever made. A year later it was cut into a TV version with a
new opening episode. The two tell the same story, and Season 2 assumes you have seen one of them. If
you want the best of both, watch the first episode of the TV version, which follows Rengoku before he
boards the train, and then the film.

Release order and the order we recommend are the same. There are no prequels or side stories to move
around.

## After the anime: where to read

Koyoharu Gotouge's manga is finished at 205 chapters. The Hashira Training Arc ends at chapter 139, and
the Infinity Castle arc begins at chapter 140, which is where the first Infinity Castle film starts. If you
cannot wait for the rest of the trilogy, chapter 140 is your starting point.

## What each arc is about

- **Season 1** is the Unwavering Resolve arc: Tanjiro's training, the Final Selection, his first missions,
  the fight with Rui on Mount Natagumo and the first meeting with the Hashira.
- **Mugen Train** puts Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke beside Rengoku against Enmu, and then against Akaza, an
  Upper Rank.
- **Entertainment District** brings in Tengen Uzui and the demon siblings Daki and Gyutaro.
- **Swordsmith Village** centres on Muichiro Tokito and Mitsuri Kanroji, and on Genya, the brother of the
  Wind Hashira.
- **Hashira Training** prepares everyone for the final battle, and ends with Muzan making his move.
- **Infinity Castle** is the start of the final battle inside Muzan's fortress.

## How long it takes

Season 1 is 26 episodes of about 24 minutes, a little over ten hours. Mugen Train is just under two hours.
The Entertainment District and Swordsmith Village arcs are 11 episodes each, around four and a half hours
apiece, with longer first and last episodes. The Hashira Training Arc is 8 episodes and about four hours,
and Infinity Castle runs two and a half hours. Together that is around 28 hours, so the whole anime fits in
a couple of busy weekends.

## A note on the films in cinemas

Demon Slayer has also had several "To the Hashira Training" and similar cinema events that showed the last
episode of one arc together with the first of the next. They are the same episodes as the TV series, so
there is nothing to hunt down if you missed them.
