Jujutsu Kaisen sorts every sorcerer and every curse into grades, and the top of that scale, special grade, is close to empty. Only four sorcerers have ever held it. This guide ranks those four by what they do on the page, explains what special grade means and how the rest of the scale works, and lists the special grade curses they are measured against.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole Jujutsu Kaisen manga, including the Shibuya Incident, the Culling Game and the final battle with Sukuna.

## How the grades work

Jujutsu Kaisen uses one scale for sorcerers and curses. From the bottom it runs grade 4, grade 3, semi-grade 2, grade 2, semi-grade 1, grade 1 and, at the top, special grade. A sorcerer's grade roughly matches the grade of curse they can be trusted to beat, so a grade 1 sorcerer is sent after grade 1 curses.

Early in the series the scale is explained with weapons. A grade 4 curse could be handled with a wooden bat, a grade 3 with a handgun, a grade 2 with a shotgun, and a grade 1 might need a tank. A special grade curse is past that point: even a carpet bombing might not be enough. Special grade sorcerers are those whose power sits on the same level.

Promotion to grade 1 and above needs recommendations from existing sorcerers and a trial period. Special grade is not a step anyone climbs to by working hard. It is a label for people who break the scale, which is why there are only four.

## How this ranking works

The four sorcerers are ranked by the feats the manga and anime show: who they beat, what they survived and what other strong characters say after watching them fight. The curses are listed after the sorcerers in the order the story meets them, because the series grades them but rarely lets them fight each other.

## Strong, but not special grade

Several characters are said to be at special grade level without the rank. **Hajime Kashimo**, a sorcerer from four hundred years ago, enters the Culling Game to fight Sukuna. **Kinji Hakari**, a third-year at Tokyo Jujutsu High, becomes close to unkillable while his domain's jackpot runs. **Yuji Itadori** ends the series with feats against Sukuna that no grade 1 could match. **Choso**, born from a special grade cursed object, Cursed Womb: Death Painting, is stronger than his grade suggests.

Then there are the people who stand outside the system. **Toji Fushiguro** has no cursed energy at all and still beat a teenage Gojo, and **Maki Zenin** reaches the same state later in the story. **Kenjaku** has lived for a thousand years in borrowed bodies and is never graded at all.

## The verdict

Gojo is the strongest special grade sorcerer and the strongest sorcerer of the modern era. Yuta is a clear second and the one who takes up Gojo's fight. Yuki's black hole shows how high her ceiling was, and Geto's army of curses made him a match for almost anyone except the two above him. On the other side of the scale, Sukuna is the special grade curse every other special grade is measured against, and the only one the story needs all its heroes to stop.
