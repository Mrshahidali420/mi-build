Demon Slayer's demons are organised like an army, and Muzan is careful about who sits where. His twelve strongest are the Twelve Kizuki, split into six Upper Ranks and six Lower Ranks, with their numbers carved into their eyes. This guide ranks the strongest demons in Demon Slayer by what they do in the finished manga, from Muzan himself down to the Lower Ranks who first test Tanjiro.

The short answer: Muzan is the strongest demon, Kokushibo is the strongest of his servants, and Doma and Akaza complete the top tier.

> Spoiler note: this ranking covers the whole manga, including the Infinity Castle and the final battle.

## How this ranking works

Demons are placed by their fights: who they killed, who it took to kill them, and how close they came to winning. The Twelve Kizuki numbers are a good guide, and the ranking mostly follows them, but a few demons do more than their number suggests. Nakime, for example, barely fights, yet her control of the Infinity Castle shapes the whole final arc. When the anime and manga differ, the manga decides.

For more than a hundred years, no Upper Rank was killed. The story's turning point is the Entertainment District, when Tengen's team breaks that record. From then on, the Upper Ranks fall one by one.

## What makes a demon strong

Every demon's strength comes from Muzan's blood and from eating people. The more humans a demon eats, and the more of Muzan's blood it receives, the stronger it grows, which is why the Upper Ranks have each lived for a century or more. Strong demons also develop a Blood Demon Art, a power unique to them, such as Doma's ice, Gyokko's pots or Nakime's control of the castle.

All of them share the same weaknesses. Sunlight destroys them, and so does a cut through the neck from a Nichirin blade, the swords forged from ore that absorbs sunlight. Wisteria poison weakens them, which is why Shinobu could fight demons she was too small to behead. The red blade of the final arc adds one more weakness: wounds from it stop regenerating, which is how the slayers finally hold Muzan in place until dawn.

## Demons who are not on the list

**Nezuko** is the most important demon in the story besides Muzan, and she becomes the first to conquer sunlight, but she never fights at an Upper Rank level. **Tamayo** and **Yushiro** are demons who broke free of Muzan's control and side with the slayers; Tamayo's drug is a big reason Muzan loses. The **Hand Demon** from Final Selection and **Kyogai**, a former Lower Rank, are Tanjiro's early tests.

## The verdict

Muzan is the strongest demon in Demon Slayer, and Kokushibo is the deadliest of those he made. Doma and Akaza killed two of the most loved characters in the series, and the lower Upper Ranks each forced the slayers into a new level of power to beat them.
