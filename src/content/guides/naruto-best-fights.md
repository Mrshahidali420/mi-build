Naruto has more great fights than almost any shonen series, and the best of them are not just about who hits harder. They are about two people who want different things and cannot both get them. This list ranks the fifteen best fights in Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, with episode numbers where they help, so you can go straight to them.

The quick answer: the final battle between Naruto and Sasuke is the best fight in the series, and Rock Lee vs Gaara is the best in the original Naruto.

> Spoiler note: this list covers the whole of Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, including the end of the war.

## How we ranked the fights

Each fight is judged on four things: the stakes for the characters, how inventive the fighting is, how well the anime animates it, and how much it changes the story. Power level matters less than meaning, which is why Shikamaru's fight with an immortal ranks above some battles with far bigger explosions. Only canon fights are included. Episode numbers come from the anime; flashback fights that are spread across many episodes are marked by arc instead.

## Just outside the top 15

**Naruto and Sasuke vs Kaguya** has the biggest stakes of all, but it is more spectacle than duel. **Sasuke vs Danzo** is a brutal Sharingan fight that shows how far Sasuke had fallen. **Gaara vs Deidara** opens Shippuden with a battle in the sky. **Kakashi vs Kakuzu**, **Killer Bee vs Kisame** and the five Kage against Madara all have strong cases. And **Hiruzen vs Orochimaru**, the Third Hokage's last stand, is the most important fight of the original series after the Valley of the End.

## The verdict

The final Naruto vs Sasuke battle is the best fight in Naruto because it is the end of a rivalry that runs through all 720 episodes. Rock Lee vs Gaara and Might Guy vs Madara prove that taijutsu fights can be the most exciting of all, and Itachi vs Sasuke is the fight that changes the most on a second watch.
