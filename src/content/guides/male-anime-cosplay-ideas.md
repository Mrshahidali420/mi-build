A good male cosplay is recognisable at a glance and comfortable enough to wear all day. Plenty of anime men dress in suits, school uniforms and simple coats, which makes them some of the easiest characters to cosplay. This guide lists twelve male anime cosplay ideas, ranked from easiest to most involved, with the key pieces, a rough budget and how long each one takes.

For more ideas that work for anyone, see the general guide to easy anime cosplay ideas.

## How we picked these costumes

Each character had to pass three tests. The outfit is built mostly from clothes you can buy or already own, with no armour and no sewing needed. The character is recognisable from outfit and hair alone. And the series is popular enough that people will know who you are at a convention, a party or a school event.

Budgets assume you buy every piece new and cheaply, in US dollars. Difficulty is rated for a complete beginner.

## Tips for male cosplay

**Start with the suit.** A plain suit covers Reigen, Light, Aki and Spike with a change of shirt and tie. If you own one, you already have four costumes.

**Spend on the hair.** A good wig in the right colour and cut does more than an expensive jacket. Style it before the day, and use a wig cap so it stays put.

**Buy the signature piece.** The Akatsuki cloak, Giyu's haori and Levi's harness are all sold ready-made. Getting the one thing everyone recognises right matters more than every other detail.

**Check prop rules.** Many conventions limit toy swords and prop weapons. Check the event's rules before bringing a katana or Zoro's three swords.

## Watch the series first

Knowing the character makes the cosplay more fun and helps you nail the poses. Each character above links to their profile and to the series page on this site, which lists the official places to watch or read it.
