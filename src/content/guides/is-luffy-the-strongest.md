Luffy has gone from a rubber boy who lost to the first strong opponent he met to one of the four Emperors of the Sea. So is he now the strongest character in One Piece? The short answer: not yet, but he is in the group that is. This guide explains what Gear 5 and the win over Kaido prove, who still sits above or beside him, and why One Piece is not finished deciding.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers One Piece up to the Elbaph arc, including Wano, Egghead and Gear 5.

## The short answer

Luffy is one of the three or four strongest active characters in One Piece. He beat Kaido, which no one had done in a straight fight for decades. But Shanks has shown more refined power, Blackbeard holds two Devil Fruits and a power made to stop Luffy's, and Imu, the hidden ruler of the world, has barely been shown. Strongest in the series means beating at least some of them, and those fights are still to come.

## How we judged it

This guide looks at fights shown in the manga and anime, and what the story says plainly about each character. Hype matters only when a fight backs it up. Characters who are dead, such as Roger and Whitebeard, are mentioned as a benchmark but not as competition, since the question is about the present day.

## Why Luffy is not the strongest yet

Three things hold him back. First, **experience**. Shanks and Blackbeard have spent decades at the top, and Luffy only reached Gear 5 at Wano. Second, **stamina**. Gear 5 is enormous, but it drains Luffy fast and leaves him weak, even aged, for a time after. Third, **the untested names**. Blackbeard's power to cancel Devil Fruits, Imu's unknown abilities and the Five Elders' regeneration at Egghead are problems Luffy has not solved.

## Why he will get there

One Piece is a story about a boy who wants to be the Pirate King, and in this world the title goes to the one who reaches Laugh Tale, the island at the end of the sea, and changes the world when he gets there. Every arc since the time skip has built toward a final war where Luffy faces Blackbeard, the Marines and the World Government. If the story follows its own pattern, Luffy ends it as the strongest, but the manga is still writing that ending.

## The verdict

Luffy is not the strongest character in One Piece yet, but he is clearly in the top tier, and beating Kaido proved it. Shanks keeps a narrow edge, Blackbeard is the rival whose powers are made for him, and Imu is the unknown at the top of the world. Expect the answer to change before One Piece ends.
