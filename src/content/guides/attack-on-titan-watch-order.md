Attack on Titan is one of the easiest big series to watch in order. There is no filler, the seasons
go in a straight line and the anime adapts the manga to its last page. The only things that cause
confusion are the way the seasons were split into parts, a handful of OVAs, and the recap films that
look like new entries but are not.

## The short answer

1. Attack on Titan (Season 1)
2. Season 2
3. Season 3
4. Season 3 Part 2
5. The Final Season
6. The Final Season Part 2
7. The Final Chapters Special 1
8. The Final Chapters Special 2

Optional extras: No Regrets (after Season 1), Lost Girls (after Season 2) and the three short OVAs.

## Why the season names are confusing

Season 3 aired in two halves a few months apart, and the Final Season ended up in three pieces: Part 1
in 2020, Part 2 in 2022, and the Final Chapters as two long specials in 2023. Some services number them
as Season 4 and split them differently, but the order never changes. If an episode list looks wrong,
follow the air years on the cards below.

## What you can skip

- **The recap films.** Crimson Bow and Arrow and Wings of Freedom retell Season 1, The Roar of Awakening
  retells Season 2, and Chronicle sums up the first three seasons. The Last Attack (2024) joins the two
  Final Chapters specials into one film for cinemas. Watch it instead of the specials if you like, not as
  well as them.
- **Attack on Titan: Junior High.** A comedy spin-off set in a school. Fun, not part of the story.
- **The chibi theatre shorts.** Comedy extras.

## After the anime

There is nothing left to read. The anime adapts all 139 chapters of Hajime Isayama's manga, and the
final special covers the last chapter, including the epilogue pages added to the final volume.

## How long it takes

The main story is about 37 hours. Season 1 is 25 episodes of about 24 minutes, a little over ten hours.
Season 2 and Season 3 are 12 episodes each, about five hours apiece, and Season 3 Part 2 is ten episodes,
around four hours. The Final Season is 16 episodes and Part 2 is 12, together a little over 11 hours, and
the two Final Chapters specials add almost two and a half hours. The OVAs add a few hours more if you want
them.
