Almost every fight in Chainsaw Man is a fight with a devil, and every devil is built on a single idea: it is the shape of something people fear. This guide explains how devils work in Chainsaw Man, the difference between devils, fiends and hybrids, and then walks through every major devil in the story, from Pochita and Makima to the Gun Devil and the Primal Devils of Hell.

The short version: the more a thing is feared, the stronger its devil. The Four Horsemen and the Primal Devils sit at the top, and the Chainsaw Devil is unique because it can erase other devils from existence.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers all of Part 1 (the Public Safety arc) and the early chapters of Part 2, including the reveals about Makima and Yoru.

## How devils work

Devils are born from fear. The Gun Devil is strong because the world fears guns, the Tomato Devil is weak because nobody is afraid of tomatoes, and the Darkness Devil is terrifying because the fear of the dark is as old as people are. A devil's name is its power.

Devils live in a cycle. When one dies on Earth it is reborn in Hell, and when it dies in Hell it is reborn on Earth. Each rebirth wipes its memory. The only devil that can break this cycle is the Chainsaw Devil: anything it eats disappears for good, along with the thing it stood for. The series hints that whole concepts, including nuclear weapons, were wiped from the world this way.

## Devils, fiends and hybrids

There are three kinds of beings with devil powers, and the story is careful to keep them apart.

**Devils** are the creatures in their own bodies. Some, like the Angel Devil, work for Public Safety; most are hunted.

**Fiends** are devils that have taken over a human corpse. They keep the devil's powers but look mostly human, with a giveaway such as Power's horns. Public Safety uses some fiends as hunters.

**Hybrids** are living humans who can switch into a devil form. Denji is one, triggered by pulling the cord in his chest. Reze and Katana Man are others. Hybrids regenerate when they drink blood, which makes them very hard to kill.

## Contracts

Humans can borrow a devil's power through a contract, paying a price the devil chooses. Aki Hayakawa carries three: the Fox Devil, which only works for people it likes; the Curse Devil, which takes years of his life; and the Future Devil, which lives in his eye. Contracts are the main way human devil hunters fight back, and their costs drive many of the story's tragedies.

## The Primal Devils and the Four Horsemen

Two groups sit above ordinary devils. The **Primal Devils**, such as Darkness and Falling, are humanity's oldest fears. Because they have never died, they have never lost strength to rebirth. The **Four Horsemen**, Control, War, Famine and Death, are named for the four riders of the end of the world. Makima, Yoru and Fami are three of them. Death, the strongest, has been talked about but not fully shown.

## The verdict

Chainsaw Man's devils are more than monsters of the week; each one turns a real fear into a fighter. The Gun Devil drives Part 1, Makima turns out to be the most dangerous devil in it, and Part 2 moves the spotlight to the Horsemen and the Primal Devils.
