In Solo Leveling, a hunter's rank decides their pay, their guild, and whether they live through a gate. S-rank is the top of the scale, and the handful of hunters who hold it are treated like national assets. This guide ranks the most important S-rank hunters in Solo Leveling by strength, explains the hunter rank system, and covers the National Level Hunters who sit above it.

The quick answer: Sung Jinwoo is officially an S-rank but outclasses everyone in the world. Below him, the American Thomas Andre is ranked first globally, followed by Liu Zhigang of China.

> Spoiler note: this ranking uses the complete Solo Leveling story, including the Monarchs' war and the ending.

## How hunter ranks work

When a person awakens as a hunter, the Hunter Association measures their mana and gives them a rank from E to S. Gates, the portals that release monsters, use the same letters, so a hunter's rank tells them which gates they can survive.

| Rank | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| E-rank | Barely stronger than a normal person |
| D-rank and C-rank | Most working hunters |
| B-rank and A-rank | Elite guild members and raid leaders |
| S-rank | The strongest few in each country, treated as national assets |
| National Level | Five hunters worldwide, each worth a country's army |

Ranks almost never change after awakening. That is what makes Jinwoo's story unusual from the first chapter.

## How this ranking works

Hunters are placed by what they do in the story: the gates they clear, the monsters they kill, and who they lose to. The world rankings named in the story are followed where they exist. Korean S-ranks who rarely fight are placed by their roles in the Jeju Island raid, the biggest joint battle they share.

## The Jeju Island raid

The Jeju Island gate is where the S-rank system meets its limit. An S-rank gate full of giant ants had already killed dozens of top hunters in three failed raids. Korea and Japan send their best together for a fourth attempt, and the Ant King tears through them, killing Goto and Min Byung-Gyu and nearly killing Cha Hae-In. Only Jinwoo's arrival saves the rest. After Jeju, the story stops treating S-rank as the ceiling.

## The verdict

Sung Jinwoo is the strongest hunter in Solo Leveling by so much that his rank stops meaning anything. Thomas Andre and Liu Zhigang lead the ordinary world rankings, and Christopher Reed's death shows that even National Level power is not enough against a Monarch.
