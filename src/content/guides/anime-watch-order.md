Most anime are easy to follow: season 1, season 2, season 3. Then there are the series that come with prequels made later, films that are part of the story, side stories, remakes and spin-offs that tell the same events another way. This guide gives the watch order for the series people ask about most, with a short answer for each and a link to a full guide where one exists.

The rule of thumb is simple: when in doubt, watch in release order. Each entry below says when that rule breaks.

## How to read a watch order

**Seasons** are the main story. Watch them all, in order.

**Canon films** continue or fill in the main story, and skipping them leaves gaps. Demon Slayer: Mugen Train and KonoSuba: Legend of Crimson are the best-known examples.

**Side-story films and OVAs** are extras, set somewhere in the timeline without changing it. Watch them when you like, or not at all.

**Recaps and remakes** retell what you have seen. They are skippable unless a later sequel follows them instead of the original, as Code Geass: Lelouch of the Re;surrection does.
