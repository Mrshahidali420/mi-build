Naruto and Naruto Shippuden together run to 720 episodes, and almost 300 of them are filler: stories made for the anime that are not in Masashi Kishimoto's manga. This Naruto filler list gives every filler episode number for both series, marks the mixed episodes you should not skip, and picks out the filler arcs that are actually worth your time.

The short version: in the original Naruto you can stop at episode 135 and go straight to Shippuden. In Shippuden, skip the episode ranges in the filler table below, and keep the mixed ones.

## What counts as filler

Episodes fall into four groups. **Manga canon** episodes adapt the manga. **Mixed canon and filler** episodes adapt the manga but add new scenes, so you should keep them. **Filler** episodes are anime-only stories you can skip without missing the plot. **Anime canon** is a rare fourth type: an anime original that fits the manga's story without changing it. Shippuden has exactly one, episode 28.

The lists below follow the standard episode-by-episode count used by fans, matched against the manga chapter by chapter.

## Naruto (2002) filler list

The original series has 220 episodes. 90 of them are filler, about 41 percent.

| Mixed canon and filler (keep) | 7, 9, 14-16, 18-21, 23-24, 27-30, 37-41, 43-47, 49, 52-60, 63, 66, 69-72, 74, 83, 98-100, 112-114, 126-127, 130-131, 141-142, 220 |
| Manga canon (keep) | 1-6, 8, 10-13, 17, 22, 25, 31-36, 42, 48, 50-51, 61-62, 64-65, 67-68, 73, 75-82, 84-96, 107-111, 115-125, 128-129, 132-135 |

The big block to notice is 136 to 219. The manga moved into its time skip after the fight at the Valley of the End, so the anime ran more than eighty episodes of its own stories while it waited. You can safely jump from episode 135 to Shippuden episode 1.

## A fast canon-only route

If you want the story with as little filler as possible, watch Naruto 1 to 135, then Shippuden 1 to 56, 72 to 90, 113 to 143, 152 to 169, 172 to 175 and 197 onward, skipping each range in the Shippuden filler table as you reach it. Keep every mixed episode. That trims roughly 290 episodes and leaves the whole manga story intact.
