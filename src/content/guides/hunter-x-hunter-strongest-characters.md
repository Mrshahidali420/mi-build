Hunter x Hunter has the most careful power system in shonen manga. Nen has rules and costs, and the strongest characters are not always the ones who win. That makes ranking them a little harder and a lot more interesting. This list ranks the fifteen strongest characters in Hunter x Hunter by what the manga and the 2011 anime show, and explains each placement.

The short answer: Meruem is the strongest, Netero is the strongest human, and adult Gon briefly reached a level that only those two can match.

> Spoiler note: this ranking covers the manga up to the Succession War arc, including the full Chimera Ant arc and the Hisoka vs Chrollo fight.

## How this ranking works

Placement goes by feats: who a character beat, who beat them, and how. Nen restrictions count, so an ability that only works on the Phantom Troupe, like Kurapika's Chain Jail, is judged with that limit in mind. One-off forms count at their peak, which is why adult Gon ranks so high. Statements of strength count for less unless a fight backs them up, which keeps characters like Ging in the middle of the list.

The Dark Continent's monsters and the Zodiacs mostly have not fought on page yet, so they are left out until the story shows more.

## Why Nen makes ranking hard

Nen abilities in Hunter x Hunter run on rules the user chooses. A stricter condition makes an ability stronger, which is why Kurapika's Chain Jail can hold the Troupe's strongest fighter but would be useless against anyone else, and why Gon's vow could create a power far beyond his normal level at the cost of everything after it. Two fighters of equal raw strength can have wildly different results depending on whose rules apply in the moment.

That is why this list puts weight on who actually beat whom. Chrollo ranks above Hisoka because he won their fight, even though many fans think Hisoka is the better fighter in a fair duel. Netero ranks above the Royal Guards because he held Meruem's full attention for an entire fight, something none of the guards could have done. Results decide the order; theories about who might win a rematch do not.

## Just outside the top 15

**Morel** and **Knov** led the palace invasion and stood against the Royal Guards. **Feitan**, **Phinks** and **Nobunaga** are among the Troupe's best fighters. **Kite** might have ranked high had Pitou not killed him so early, and **Pariston** and **Beyond Netero** clearly hold great power that the story has barely shown. **Biscuit Krueger** in her true form is one of the toughest fighters the kids ever meet.

## The verdict

Meruem is the strongest character in Hunter x Hunter, and the story built the whole Chimera Ant arc to prove it. Netero is the best humanity can offer, adult Gon is a terrifying one-time peak, and the Royal Guards round out the top tier. Below them, the Zoldycks and the Phantom Troupe fight in a tight middle where the cleverest use of Nen often beats raw strength.
