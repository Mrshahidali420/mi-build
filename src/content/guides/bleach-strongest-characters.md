Bleach spends its whole run raising the ceiling. Every arc brings a new tier of enemy, from captains to Espada to Yhwach's Sternritter, and the final war finally shows what the oldest powers in Soul Society can do. This ranking places the fifteen strongest characters in Bleach by what the manga and the Thousand-Year Blood War anime show them doing.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole Bleach manga, including the Thousand-Year Blood War and its ending.

## How this ranking works

Characters are judged at their peak in the main Bleach manga, which the Thousand-Year Blood War anime follows closely. Filler arcs from the original anime, films and the light novels are left out. Placement goes by feats: who they beat, who beat them, and what the story says plainly about their strength.

Bleach has a lot of power that is hinted at and never shown. When a character's strength rests mostly on hype, the entry says so and they rank below characters who prove it in a fight.

## How Bleach measures power

Bleach never uses numbers. Strength shows through spiritual pressure, reiatsu, which a much stronger fighter can use to crush weaker ones just by being near them, and through released forms. A Soul Reaper's Zanpakuto has a first release, Shikai, and a second, Bankai, which only the very best ever reach. Arrancar have their own release, and the Quincy of the final war have Schrift, a power given to them by Yhwach and named with a letter.

That makes Bankai a useful line in any ranking. Characters who fight without one, or lose theirs, drop sharply, which is why the Quincy plan to steal Bankai in the final war is so devastating. It also means a character who hides their Bankai, as Kenpachi and Urahara do for most of the series, is easy to underrate until the final arc.

## Just outside the top 15

**Uryu Ishida** lands the arrow that stops Yhwach's Almighty for a moment, which is the most important single shot of the war, but not proof of raw strength. **Askin Nakk Le Vaar** pushes Urahara, Grimmjow and Yoruichi together. **Starrk**, the Primera Espada, and **Grimmjow** are the strongest Espada after Ulquiorra. **Toshiro Hitsugaya** reaches an adult form of his Bankai in the final war. **Yoruichi** is the fastest fighter in Soul Society and a match for many names above.

## The verdict

Yhwach is the strongest character in Bleach, and the story needs three of its biggest names to stop him. Ichigo ends the series above Aizen because he beat him at his peak, and Aizen stays the most dangerous mind in the story. Yamamoto is the strongest captain, and Kenpachi, once his Bankai is free, is the strongest fighter who only ever wants a good fight.
