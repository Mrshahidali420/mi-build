Super Saiyan is the most copied idea in anime: a hero pushed past breaking point, golden hair, a burst of light, and suddenly the fight is different. Dragon Ball has spent forty years building on it. This guide explains every Super Saiyan form in the order the story introduces it, what each one does, who reached it first, and what is known about its power level.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball Super (anime and manga), the recent films, Dragon Ball GT and Dragon Ball Daima.

## How Super Saiyan works

A Super Saiyan is a Saiyan who has broken through a limit, usually in a moment of extreme anger. In Dragon Ball Z the first transformation is treated as a legend that appears once in a thousand years, until Goku makes it real on Namek. After that, Saiyans learn to reach it through training and keep pushing it further.

Each step has the same pattern. A new form appears in a desperate fight, it carries a cost such as energy drain or lost speed, and the next saga shows a character mastering that cost.

## Power levels and multipliers

The only official number tied to a form is the Daizenshuu's statement that Super Saiyan multiplies power by fifty, with Goku going from 3 million to 150 million against Frieza. After that the story stopped using power levels. Multipliers you see online for Super Saiyan 2, 3, God and Blue come from video games, magazines and fan math, and they disagree with each other. Treat them as guesses.

## Forms used by others

**Super Saiyan Rosé** is Goku Black's version of Super Saiyan Blue, coloured pink, in the Future Trunks arc. **Kaioken** is not a Saiyan form but a technique Goku stacks on top of Super Saiyan Blue. **Fusions** such as Gogeta, Vegito and Gotenks can use the same forms, and their combined power is greater than either half's.

## The verdict

Super Saiyan Blue is the strongest true Super Saiyan form in the main story. The forms that beat it, Ultra Instinct, Ultra Ego and Beast, are each something else: a state of mind, a god's mindset and a hidden power. That change says a lot about modern Dragon Ball. Raw transformation is no longer enough; the strongest fighters are the ones who understand themselves.
