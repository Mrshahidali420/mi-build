Jujutsu Kaisen is short and simple to follow: three TV seasons and one film that matters, with two
more cinema releases that repeat what the TV series already shows. The one real question is where to put
JUJUTSU KAISEN 0, and the answer most fans give is right after Season 1.

## The short answer

1. Jujutsu Kaisen Season 1
2. JUJUTSU KAISEN 0 (film)
3. Jujutsu Kaisen Season 2
4. Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3: The Culling Game Part 1

## Where JJK 0 fits

JUJUTSU KAISEN 0 is a prequel. It adapts the four-chapter series Gege Akutami published before the
main manga, about Yuta Okkotsu, a student haunted by the curse of the girl he loved as a child. In time
order it comes first, but it was made after Season 1, and it plays best there.

Season 1 mentions Yuta and the second-year students without showing them, so the film fills a gap you
already feel. More importantly, its villain, Suguru Geto, is at the heart of Season 2, which opens
with his school days alongside Gojo. Watching the film between the two seasons means you meet him at
the right moment.

If you do start with the film, you will not be lost. You will just meet the world through a different
lead, and Season 1 will feel like a soft restart.

## After the anime: where to read

Gege Akutami's manga ended in 2024 at chapter 271, so if you want the ending now, it is all there.
Season 2 stops at the end of the Shibuya Incident, which is chapter 136. If you have watched up to
Season 2, start the manga at chapter 137. If you have watched Season 3 Part 1, you can read on from where
it stops, or start at chapter 137 to see the Culling Game in full detail.

## What each season covers

- **Season 1** follows Yuji from the day he eats Sukuna's finger through his first missions, the fight
  with the cursed spirit Mahito, the Kyoto Goodwill Event between the two jujutsu schools, and the Death
  Painting arc that closes the season.
- **JUJUTSU KAISEN 0** is Yuta's year at Jujutsu High and his clash with Suguru Geto on Christmas Eve.
- **Season 2** opens with Gojo and Geto's own school days, then spends most of its episodes on the
  Shibuya Incident, which runs almost in real time over one Halloween night.
- **Season 3 Part 1** starts in the ruins of Shibuya and moves into the Culling Game.

## How long it takes

Season 1 is 24 episodes of about 24 minutes, a little under ten hours. JUJUTSU KAISEN 0 adds an hour and
three quarters. Season 2 is 23 episodes, roughly nine hours, and Season 3 Part 1 is 12 episodes, close to
five hours. All together the main story is about 25 hours, which makes it one of the easier big series to
catch up on before a new season airs.
