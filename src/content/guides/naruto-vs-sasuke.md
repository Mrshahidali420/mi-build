Naruto vs Sasuke is the rivalry that holds the whole series together. It starts with two academy kids who cannot stand each other and ends, fifteen years of manga and 720 episodes later, with them lying side by side at the Valley of the End. This guide goes through every fight between Naruto and Sasuke in order, with episode numbers and results, and answers the question every fan asks: who is stronger?

The short version: they fight five times. Sasuke wins the first full fight at the Valley of the End, Naruto wins the final one, and the rest are stopped before anyone wins.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers the whole of Naruto and Naruto Shippuden, including the final battle.

## How the rivalry works

From the start, Sasuke is the genius and Naruto the dropout. Sasuke is driven by revenge on his brother Itachi; Naruto by the wish to be acknowledged, and later by his promise to bring Sasuke home. Every fight between them is really an argument about those two goals. The power gap swings back and forth: Sasuke is ahead for almost all of the original series and early Shippuden, and Naruto only draws level near the end of the war.

Late in the story, the series reveals why it feels like fate. Naruto and Sasuke are the reincarnations of Asura and Indra, the sons of the Sage of Six Paths, whose rivalry has repeated through history, including in Hashirama and Madara.

That leaves the head-to-head record level at one win each, with three fights unfinished. What decides the rivalry is that Naruto wins the last one, the only fight where both are at their peak.

## Rasengan and Chidori

Almost every fight between them ends the same way: Naruto's Rasengan against Sasuke's Chidori. Both techniques come from their teachers. The Rasengan was created by Naruto's father, Minato, and taught to Naruto by Jiraiya; the Chidori was created by Kakashi and taught to Sasuke. So each clash is also a meeting of two lines of teachers, and the techniques grow with the boys. On the hospital roof they are simple. At the first Valley of the End they carry the Nine-Tails and the Cursed Mark. In the final battle they become a Rasengan made with every tailed beast's chakra and Indra's Arrow, a Chidori charged through a Susanoo.

## The Seal of Reconciliation

At the ninja academy, students learn that when a sparring match ends, the two fighters link two fingers in a hand sign to show they are still comrades. As boys, Naruto and Sasuke refuse to make it with each other. At the end of the final battle, lying on the ground with one arm each, they finally make the sign together, with the two hands they have left. It is the series' last word on the rivalry: not a winner, but two friends who stopped fighting.

## After Shippuden

In Boruto the two are allies, Naruto as the Seventh Hokage and Sasuke as the village's protector from the shadows. They fight side by side against the Otsutsuki rather than against each other, and both pay heavily for it. ## Who is stronger?

At the end of Shippuden, Naruto is stronger, by a small margin. In Six Paths Sage Mode with all nine tailed beasts' chakra, he has more power and far more stamina, and he wins the final battle. Sasuke's Rinnegan gives him tools Naruto lacks, like swapping places with Amenotejikara and casting genjutsu on the tailed beasts, so on skill and versatility he is at least Naruto's equal. Our [Naruto strongest characters](/guides/naruto-strongest-characters) ranking puts them second and third, just below Kaguya.

## The verdict

Sasuke led for most of the story, and Naruto caught him at the end. The rivalry finishes as it should: Naruto wins the last fight, not by being stronger alone, but by refusing to stop.
