A power level is a number a scouter reads off a fighter, and for a few years of Dragon Ball Z it was the clearest way to see who would win. This guide explains Dragon Ball power levels, lists the official readings from the Saiyan arrival to the battle on Namek, and explains why the numbers fade out once Super Saiyan arrives.

The short version: the manga reads Goku at 334 when Dragon Ball Z begins and at 180,000 when he fights Captain Ginyu. Frieza states his power as 530,000 in his first form and over a million in his second, and after that the story stops counting.

## What a power level is

In Japanese the term is "sentō-ryoku", combat power. Raditz and the other Saiyans wear scouters, eyepieces that measure the energy in a living being and show it as a number. At first it works perfectly: Raditz scans a farmer with a shotgun and reads 5, then scans Piccolo and Goku and decides neither is a threat.

The number is a measure of energy at that moment, not a fixed rating. Goku and his friends learn to hide their power, and Gohan's level jumps with his emotions. That is why the scouter keeps getting the fights wrong, and why it eventually breaks under readings it cannot handle.

## Where the numbers come from

There are two sources of power levels, and it helps to keep them apart. **The manga** states a small set of numbers on the page, mostly in the Saiyan and Frieza arcs. **The official guidebooks**, chiefly the Daizenshuu, list many more, including the famous Super Saiyan figures that never appear in the story. This guide marks each number with where it comes from. Video game and anime filler numbers are left out.

## Why the numbers stop

By the end of the Frieza arc, power levels had done their job. The heroes now sense energy directly, villains hide their strength, and transformations multiply power so much that a number stops meaning anything. The scouter stops being a storytelling tool, and the later arcs, from the Androids to Majin Buu and on into Dragon Ball Super, never bring the numbers back in canon.

What replaces them is a ladder of forms and rivals: Super Saiyan, Super Saiyan 2 and 3, God, Blue, Ultra Instinct and Ultra Ego. Who beats whom is the only reliable measure.
