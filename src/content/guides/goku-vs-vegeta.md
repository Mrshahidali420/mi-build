Goku vs Vegeta is the rivalry at the heart of Dragon Ball. It started with an invasion in 1989, became a grudging friendship, and has kept going through every transformation since. This guide walks through every real fight between them, compares their best feats and peak forms, and gives a clear verdict on who wins.

The verdict, up front: **Goku wins, but only just.** He has the better head-to-head record and has reached every major form first. Vegeta is closer than he has ever been, and in some arcs of Dragon Ball Super he arguably leads.

> Spoiler note: this guide covers Dragon Ball Z, Dragon Ball Super (anime and manga) and Dragon Ball Daima.

## The rules of the fight

- **Both at their peak:** Goku with Ultra Instinct, Vegeta with Ultra Ego, as shown in the Dragon Ball Super manga.
- **No fusion:** Gogeta and Vegito are both of them, so they do not count for either side.
- **Feats only:** what the anime and manga show, not video games or guidebook guesses.
- **A real fight:** both trying to win, no holding back, no outside help.

## Every Goku vs Vegeta fight

| Fight | Where | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Saiyans arrive | Dragon Ball Z, Saiyan arc | Goku's side wins; Goku lets Vegeta escape |
| Majin Vegeta | Dragon Ball Z, Buu arc | Even, ended by Vegeta's sneak attack |
| Training under Whis | Dragon Ball Super | Sparring; no decisive result |
| God forms and beyond | Dragon Ball Super manga | Rival paths; no decisive result |

**The Saiyan arc** is the only fight where both are truly trying to kill each other. Goku pushes Kaio-ken as far as his body can take it and matches Vegeta's Galick Gun with a Kamehameha. Vegeta turns into a Great Ape and nearly crushes him. It takes Gohan, Krillin and Yajirobe together to end it. Goku's side wins, and Goku chooses to spare him.

**Majin Vegeta** is the rematch Vegeta wanted. He lets Babidi control him just to get a real fight, and at Super Saiyan 2 they are close to even. Vegeta ends it by knocking Goku out from behind so he can face Buu alone. Later in the arc, after seeing Goku's Super Saiyan 3, Vegeta admits the truth: Goku is number one.

**Dragon Ball Super** turns the rivalry into training. Both learn from Whis, both reach Super Saiyan God and Blue, and then they split. Goku chases Ultra Instinct; Vegeta, refusing to copy him, takes Beerus's path to Ultra Ego.

## Their forms, side by side

| Form | Goku | Vegeta |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Super Saiyan | First, against Frieza on Namek | Reaches it in space during the Android wait |
| Super Saiyan 2 | During the seven years after Cell | Reaches it by the Buu arc, shown as Majin Vegeta |
| Super Saiyan 3 | Against Fat Buu in the Buu arc | Reaches it in Dragon Ball Daima |
| Super Saiyan God and Blue | First to God; both reach Blue under Whis | Reaches Blue alongside Goku |
| Peak path | Ultra Instinct | Ultra Ego |

The pattern is clear. Goku reaches each step first, and Vegeta catches up. The only real break comes in Super, where Vegeta refuses to follow Goku into Ultra Instinct and chooses a path that is his alone.

## Why the rivalry matters

Vegeta's whole story is told through Goku. He arrives as a villain who sees a low-class Saiyan as beneath him, becomes an ally only because they share an enemy, and lets Babidi corrupt him because he cannot stand being second. His sacrifice against Fat Buu and his "you are number one" admission are the turning points of his character. Goku, for his part, keeps treating Vegeta as the rival he enjoys fighting most, which is part of why Vegeta never quite forgives him.

In Dragon Ball Super the rivalry finally becomes friendly. They train together, fuse when they must, and argue like brothers. But the question of who is stronger is still the thing that drives them both.

## Where each one has the edge

**Goku's edges:** experience at the very top, a head-to-head record in his favour, and Ultra Instinct's ability to dodge without thinking. He has also been the first to every major form, from Super Saiyan to Super Saiyan 4 in Daima.

**Vegeta's edges:** Ultra Ego grows stronger as he takes damage, which suits a long fight, and he has a talent for unique techniques like Forced Spirit Fission. His pride is both his weakness and his engine; every time Goku pulls ahead, Vegeta trains harder.

## The verdict

Goku wins a full-power fight more often than not. His record, his consistent lead in transformations and Vegeta's own admission in the Buu arc all point the same way. But the margin is the smallest it has ever been, and Ultra Ego was built for exactly this kind of fight. Ask again after the next Dragon Ball Super arc and the answer may change.
