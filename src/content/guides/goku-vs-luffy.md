Goku vs Luffy is the most asked crossover fight in anime, and for good reason. Both are cheerful, food-loving heroes who get stronger through every fight, both lead two of the best-selling manga ever written, and both have a new god-tier form fans love to argue about. This guide puts Gear 5 Luffy against Ultra Instinct Goku and settles who would win, with both sides' best feats and the rules of the fight laid out.

The verdict, up front: **Goku wins, comfortably.** Luffy's best feats are enormous for One Piece, but Dragon Ball works on a scale of planets and universes, and that gap decides the fight.

> Spoiler note: this guide uses One Piece up to the Egghead arc, including Gear 5, and Dragon Ball Super up to the Tournament of Power and beyond.

## The rules of the fight

To keep this fair, both fighters are taken at their peak and judged only by what their own series shows.

- **Goku:** Dragon Ball Super, with Ultra Instinct and every technique he has shown.
- **Luffy:** One Piece up to the Egghead arc, with Gear 5 and his full Haki.
- **Setting:** an empty field with nothing to use or hide behind. No allies, no preparation.
- **Win condition:** knockout or death. Both are fighting to win from the first second.
- **Feats only:** no video games, no non-canon movies, no "plot armour" arguments.

## How the fight plays out

**Speed.** This is the first and biggest problem for Luffy. Goku has crossed vast distances in moments since Dragon Ball Z, and Ultra Instinct makes him react before an attack is even fully thrown. Luffy is fast for One Piece, and Observation Haki lets him see a moment ahead, but Goku's reaction is automatic and faster still.

**Power.** Luffy's strongest punches flatten buildings and drive Kaido through the ground. Goku's ordinary blasts in Dragon Ball Z already destroy mountains, and in Super he trades blows with gods. The difference is not one or two levels; it is several orders of size.

**Durability.** Rubber protects Luffy from blunt hits and electricity, which helps against most One Piece enemies. It does nothing against the Kamehameha, which is pure energy. Haki hardens Luffy's body, but it was never designed to block a planet-level blast.

**Tricks.** Gear 5 is the most creative power in One Piece, and Luffy's toon logic could surprise Goku for a moment. But Goku has fought shape-shifters, time stoppers and gods. Surprise buys Luffy seconds, not a win.

## Where Luffy has the edge

Fairness matters, so here is what Luffy does better. He is far more inventive in the moment, which Gear 5 only amplifies. His Conqueror's Haki can knock out weaker fighters just by being released, and his willpower has carried him through fights he should have lost. Against early Goku, from the original Dragon Ball, those edges would win him the fight. Against Dragon Ball Z Goku from the Frieza arc onward, they are not enough.

## Common arguments for Luffy, answered

**"Gear 5 is toon force, so Luffy can do anything."** Gear 5 lets Luffy treat the world like a cartoon, but the series still shows limits. He tires out, his form wears off, and he never shows power beyond the island scale of his fight with Kaido. Toon force in One Piece is a style of fighting, not a guarantee of winning.

**"Conqueror's Haki would knock Goku out."** Conqueror's Haki only knocks out people far weaker in will and strength than the user. Kaido, Big Mom and every top fighter in One Piece shrug it off. Goku, who has faced down gods, would not fall to it.

**"Luffy is rubber, so punches do nothing."** Rubber absorbs blunt force in One Piece, but Haki-coated blows break through it, and Goku's punches carry energy far beyond anything Haki produces. His ki blasts are not blunt force at all.

**"Luffy always wins because of his will."** Willpower is real in One Piece; it is how Luffy survives fights he should lose. But it works inside his world's scale. It cannot make up the difference between flattening a building and shaking a universe.

## Lower versions of the fight

The matchup changes a lot if you pick earlier versions of Goku. **Kid Goku** from the start of Dragon Ball loses to Gear 5 Luffy, and probably to Wano Luffy too. **Goku at the end of Dragon Ball**, after the Piccolo Jr. fight, is already very strong, but Gear 5's creativity and Haki give Luffy a real chance. **Goku on Namek**, fighting Frieza, is the point where the gap opens for good: the fight ends with a planet being destroyed, and Luffy has nothing to answer that.

## The verdict

Goku wins. At their peaks the gap in speed, power and scale is too large for creativity or Haki to close. Luffy is the strongest fighter in One Piece, and Goku is not even the strongest in Dragon Ball, which tells you how differently the two worlds are built.
