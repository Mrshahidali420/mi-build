Fate has more than twenty anime, and they do not come with a number on the box. The good news is that
the core of the franchise is only five entries long: two seasons of Unlimited Blade Works, three
Heaven's Feel films, and two seasons of Fate/Zero. Everything else is a side story, an alternate world
or a separate branch that you can pick up later, in almost any order.

This guide gives the order we recommend for a first watch, then the full release order, then what each
spin-off is and whether you can skip it.

## The short answer

1. Fate/stay night: Unlimited Blade Works (2014), both seasons
2. Fate/stay night: Heaven's Feel, films I, II and III
3. Fate/Zero, both seasons
4. Anything else you like: Lord El-Melloi II's Case Files, Fate/Apocrypha, Fate/Grand Order, Fate/strange Fake

## Why Unlimited Blade Works comes first

Fate began as a visual novel, Fate/stay night (2004), with three story routes through the same Holy
Grail War: Fate (Saber), Unlimited Blade Works (Rin) and Heaven's Feel (Sakura). Each route answers
questions the others leave open, and the anime adapted them separately, years apart.

Unlimited Blade Works is the best door in. It explains the war, the Servants and the Command Seals
without assuming anything, and its first episode is a prologue built for newcomers. Heaven's Feel then
takes the same cast into much darker ground, and it works best when you already know who everyone is.

Fate/Zero is the hard one to place. It was made first and tells the earlier war, so a straight time
order puts it at the front. But it gives away who several Servants are, what happened to Shirou's
family and why Kirei Kotomine matters, and stay night is written to reveal those things slowly. That is
why we put it third. If you already know the spoilers, starting with Fate/Zero is fine.

## What you can skip

- **The 2010 Unlimited Blade Works film.** It squeezes the whole route into one movie. The 2014 series
  tells the same story properly.
- **Fate/EXTRA Last Encore.** A standalone Holy Grail War fought inside a digital world on the Moon,
  with its own cast. Fine on its own terms, not needed for anything else.
- **Fate/kaleid liner Prisma Illya.** A magical girl spin-off with several seasons and films. Fun, but
  in a world of its own.
- **Today's Menu for the Emiya Family.** Thirteen short cooking episodes with the stay night cast. A
  treat once you have finished the main story, and spoiler-free.
- **Fate/Zero Cafe and the Einzbern Counseling Room shorts.** Comedy extras.
